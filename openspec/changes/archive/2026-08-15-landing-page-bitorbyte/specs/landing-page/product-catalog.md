# Product Catalog Specification

## Overview
The product catalog must present bitORbyte's offerings not as software packages, but as solutions to specific daily operational pain points.

## Requirements

### 1. Product Definitions
Each product must be presented with a "Day-to-Day" focus:

- **Point of Sale (POS)**:
    - Focus: Speed of sale, accuracy of payment.
    - Formats: Desktop, SaaS, or Hybrid.
    - Value: "Sells fast, charges accurately, zero friction."
- **E-commerce**:
    - Focus: 24/7 availability, simple management.
    - Format: SaaS.
    - Value: "Your digital storefront as simple as your physical one."
- **Inventory Management**:
    - Focus: Stock visibility, error reduction.
    - Formats: Desktop, SaaS, or Hybrid.
    - Value: "Know exactly what you have and what you lack."

### 2. Presentation Logic
- **Anti-Bloat Guarantee**: Every product card must include a mention that there are no "extra modules" or hidden fees for unused features.
- **Ownership**: Highlight that the client is the owner of the resulting process.

### 3. UI Components
- Use `UKit Cards` with subtle hover effects to draw attention to the specific solution.
- Clear distinction between the different deployment formats (Desktop/SaaS/Hybrid) using badges or labels.
