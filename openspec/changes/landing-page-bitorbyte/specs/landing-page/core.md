# Core Landing Page Specification

## Overview
The core landing page serves as the primary gateway for bitORbyte. It must establish a feeling of professionalism, trust, and empowerment, moving away from "cheap services" toward "technological sovereignty."

## Requirements

### 1. Visual Identity & Tone
- **Tone**: Professional, accessible, transparent, and empowering.
- **Visual Style**: Minimalist Corporate. High use of white space to avoid cognitive overload.
- **Key Messaging**: Focus on "Technology adapting to your business and budget."

### 2. Layout Structure
- **Header**: Sticky navigation with logo and a clear CTA ("Start Free Exploration").
- **Hero Section**: 
    - High-impact headline (H1).
    - Supportive sub-headline emphasizing SMEs.
    - Primary action button.
- **Manifesto Section**: A comparison area (Traditional vs. bitORbyte) highlighting the removal of dependency and hidden costs.
- **Footer**: Simple contact info, copyright, and a final lead-capture form.

### 3. Technical Constraints
- **Responsive**: Fully compatible with mobile, tablet, and desktop.
- **Framework**: Built using UIKit 3 components for consistency and speed.
- **Performance**: Fast load times for users with limited connectivity.
