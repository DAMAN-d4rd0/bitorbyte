## Why

bitORbyte needs a digital presence that communicates its unique value proposition: providing professional technology consulting to SMEs without the prohibitive costs typically associated with large firms. The goal is to shift the narrative from "cheap software" to "technological sovereignty," empowering small business owners to own their processes and products.

## What Changes

- Create a modern, high-conversion landing page using HTML, CSS (UIKit), and Vanilla JavaScript.
- Implement a visual "infographic" flow to explain the 3-step free service model (Exploration $\rightarrow$ Evaluation $\rightarrow$ Implementation).
- Develop a product catalog focusing on "Day-to-Day" utility for POS, E-commerce, and Inventory systems.
- Apply a "Corporate Trust" visual identity to eliminate the perception of high costs while maintaining a professional image.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `landing-page/core`: Definition of the page structure, visual identity, and the "Sovereignty" narrative.
- `landing-page/value-infographic`: Specification for the interactive visual flow of the free service model.
- `landing-page/product-catalog`: Definition of the product offerings focused on SME daily operations.

### Modified Capabilities
- None

## Impact

- New frontend assets (HTML, CSS, JS).
- Integration of UIKit framework.
- Creation of a new public-facing communication channel for the business.
